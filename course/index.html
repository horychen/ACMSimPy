How It Works

A typical lecture flow

Step 01
Before class: Pre-Survey
Start the server with python server.py. Students scan the QR code on their phones and fill out the background survey. Their responses are saved as JSON files for AI analysis.
Step 02
AI analyzes responses
Feed all survey JSONs to Claude. It identifies knowledge gaps, common misconceptions, and the class's math/programming comfort level. This shapes the lecture content.
Step 03
During lecture: Live Q&A
Open the Teacher Panel on your laptop. Push questions to students' phones in real-time. See their answers stream in live. Use templates for quick polls (Yes/No, A/B/C/D, scales).
Step 04
After lecture: Save & analyze
Click "Save session" in the Teacher Panel. All live Q&A history is written to disk. Use Claude to analyze engagement, identify who understood the material, and plan the next lecture.
Step 05
Iterate and improve
Each lecture builds on the previous analysis. The course adapts to your specific students' needs, creating a feedback loop between teaching, assessment, and AI-assisted planning.
